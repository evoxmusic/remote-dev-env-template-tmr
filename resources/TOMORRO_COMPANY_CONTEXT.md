## What is Tomorro?

**Tomorro** (formerly Leeway) is a Paris-based SaaS company that builds an **AI-powered Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) platform**. It helps businesses manage every stage of their contracts — from creation and negotiation to approval, signature, archiving, and monitoring.

- **Legal name:** Tomorro SAS
- **Headquarters:** 46 Rue Notre-Dame des Victoires, 75002 Paris, France
- **Website:** [tomorro.com](https://www.tomorro.com)
- **App:** [app.tomorro.com](https://app.tomorro.com)
- **Founded:** France-based startup, now serving 2,000+ companies
- **Rating:** 4.6/5 on G2 (141+ reviews)
- **Certification:** ISO-27001 certified
- **Sustainability:** Dedicates 1% of revenue to carbon dioxide elimination via Stripe Climate

## Core Product: Oro — AI Legal Assistant

**Oro** is Tomorro's flagship AI assistant, embedded throughout the platform. It accelerates the entire contract lifecycle.

### Oro Capabilities

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI-assisted review** | Analyzes contracts against your company's contract policy, flags risks, suggests changes. Gives operational teams autonomy while maintaining legal oversight. |
| **Smart chat** | Ask questions about any single contract or your entire contract library. Get instant answers without reading full documents. |
| **Data extraction** | Automatically extracts key data points (dates, amounts, parties, clauses) from contracts. 92% accuracy. |
| **Contract summaries** | Generates team-specific summaries — legal gets different highlights than finance or operations. |
| **Smart writing** | Translates, generates, and reformulates contract content. Creates custom clauses and templates on demand. |

### Performance

- **53% shorter** contract negotiation times with Oro (measured across 12,248 contracts)
- **92% accuracy** on data extraction

### AI Engines

Tomorro offers two AI engines — users can activate one or both:
- **Azure OpenAI** (default) — high-performance, hosted in Europe, GDPR and AI Act compliant
- **Mistral** — French sovereign alternative, also hosted in Europe

### Pricing Model

Tomorro charges only for **Admin** and **Manager** (creator) roles. **Viewers** and **co-contractors** (external parties) are free and unlimited. This means the whole company can access contracts without per-seat costs for read-only users.

- 12-month minimum commitment
- Annual billing (credit card, bank transfer, or SEPA direct debit)
- Free 2-week trial available
